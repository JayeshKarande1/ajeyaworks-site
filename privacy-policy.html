Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

AjeyaWorks ("we", "our", or "us") builds Android applications and related web services. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile applications or visit ajeyaworks.in.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the specific app you use, we may process:

  • Information you provide: Such as your email address or support messages if you contact us or create an account in an app that requires sign-in.
  • App diagnostics & crash logs: Basic technical data (device model, OS version, crash traces) used solely to fix bugs and improve app stability.
  • Third-party services: Some apps may use Google Play Services, Firebase, or advertising SDKs, which process data according to their own privacy policies.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information strictly to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve app features.
  • Respond to user support requests.
  • Monitor app stability, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is only shared with trusted infrastructure providers (such as Google Play Services) when required to operate the app, or if required by law.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain data only as long as necessary for the purposes described above. If an app supports user accounts, you can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by following the instructions on our Account Deletion page.

5. Children's Privacy

Unless explicitly stated for a specific app, our applications are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as we release new apps or features. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at support@ajeyaworks.in or ajeyaworks@gmail.com.
