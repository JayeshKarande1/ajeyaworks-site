Account & Data Deletion

AjeyaWorks Apps

If you use an AjeyaWorks Android application that includes user accounts, you can request permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to support@ajeyaworks.in (or ajeyaworks@gmail.com) from the email address registered with your account.
  2. Include the app name and your registered email or user ID in the message.
  3. We will confirm your request and delete your account data within 7 business days.

What Data Is Deleted

  • Your account profile, login credentials, and user-generated app data stored on our servers.
  • Any non-essential preferences tied to your account.

Minimal records may be retained only if strictly required for fraud prevention, billing records, or legal compliance.
